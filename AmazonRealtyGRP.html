About Amazon Realty Group

Founded in 2008, Amazon Realty Group LLC specializes in full-service property management across New York City. We deliver a high level of professionalism and personalized attention to every client. Our hands-on approach ensures budgets are maintained, costs are controlled, and issues are resolved swiftly and effectively. We foster a safe, family-like environment in every building, using best-in-class practices and a minimum three-bid policy to secure the highest quality at the best price.

Clara Sokol, a New York State licensed broker, leads a proven portfolio of 9 rental properties (4 to 41 units), 3 commercial spaces, and 4 newly developed condominiums (5 to 8 units each). With 17 years of experience in administrative and operational leadership, we bring deep expertise in compliance, tenant relations, and financial oversight. References from current and past clients are available upon request.
